Windows

Window Systems Engineered for Comfort

European-style uPVC and aluminum profiles, matched to Missouri's climate and professionally installed.

uPVC Product Family

Three depths of the same idea: a well-sealed frame

Every uPVC series shares the same multi-chamber construction principle — more chambers and greater frame depth mean better insulation and a quieter home. The right series depends on the opening and how much performance the room needs.

58

Classic Dependability

2-1/4" depth · 3-chamber

The standard window and door layout for typical residential openings. A budget-conscious profile without compromising frame integrity.

71

High Thermal Efficiency

2-3/4" depth · 6-chamber

Advanced 6-chamber construction for diverse climate zones that need real heating and cooling retention through Missouri's seasons.

80

Ultra-Premium Shield

3-1/8" depth · 7-chamber

A triple-gasket weatherseal system with an additional central gasket, built for the least air leakage and the most sound deadening.

The Cross-Section

What's actually inside the frame

Every profile is multi-chambered uPVC with steel-reinforced corners, a warm-edge spacer bar, and an insulated glass unit sealed into place — not a hollow shell painted to look substantial.

Architectural Aluminum

For larger openings and heavier glass

Series 38 and 46M are non-thermal aluminum solutions suited to interior vestibules and partitions. For exterior applications, the FT 72 thermal-break system uses polyamide strips to deliver structural stability for heavier glazed configurations — narrow sightlines, high mechanical strength.

Premium Sliding Systems

Wide openings, engineered to move smoothly

  • HS 180 Lift-and-Slide

    Thermal-break aluminum, sashes up to 10.5 ft (3.2 m) tall and 880 lbs (400 kg), with exceptional wind and water tightness.

  • ISS 147 Parallel-Sliding

    uPVC with active double-contour sealing — continuous compression, no lifting required to slide. Sashes up to 6.5 × 8.2 ft.

Testing & Engineering
