Entry Doors

Three constructions, one standard of fit

Every entry door is built around a steel-reinforced frame and a multi-point locking system. The panel construction is where the three lines differ.

Steel Panel

Series 71 / 80

A steel panel over expanded polystyrene fill, with a 3-chamber insulating glass unit and stainless steel or painted galvanized decorative molding.

  • 71 or 80 mm frame depth
  • 3-section rebated hinges
  • Multi-point lock
  • Threshold with thermal break

PVC Panel GLS

Series 71 / 80

A laminated PVC sandwich panel with a glazed insert, combining the look of natural materials with modern insulation.

  • 32–52 mm frame depth
  • 1- or 2-chamber glass unit
  • Multi-point lock
  • Surface-mounted or roller hinges

PVC Panel

Series 71

A PVC sandwich panel with integrated double-glazing and a mullion profile, suited to both single doors and paired patio-style configurations.

  • 24–42 mm frame depth
  • Single-point, latch, or multi-point lock
  • Universal thermal-insert threshold

Design & Finish

First impressions matter

Decorative beveled and frosted glass inserts, moldings, and a wide color range let the door read as a design feature, not an afterthought.

  • White (RAL 9003M), Anthracite Grey, and Black (RAL 9005S) finishes
  • Wood-look laminations: Golden Oak, Nussbaum, Dark Oak, Mahogany
  • Standard and premium handle sets, in matched heights

Patio & Sliding Doors

Bring the outdoors in

Wide sliding systems create a seamless connection to a patio or backyard without sacrificing security or energy efficiency.

  • HS 180 Lift-and-Slide

    Thermal-break aluminum. Sashes up to 10.5 ft (3.2 m) tall and 880 lbs (400 kg), with exceptional wind and water tightness.

  • ISS 147 Parallel-Sliding

    uPVC with active double-contour sealing and continuous compression — no lifting required to slide. Sashes up to 6.5 × 8.2 ft.
