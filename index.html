Why Elegant Windows & Doors

Most of what determines whether a window performs for twenty years happens before it's ever installed.

European-engineered systems

uPVC profiles from a 3-chamber standard build up to a 7-chamber, triple-gasket system, plus aluminum for larger openings and heavier glazed configurations. Built for thermal performance, not just appearance.

Selected for this climate

Missouri swings from humid summers to hard freezes. Frame depth, chamber count, and glass packages are chosen with that range in mind, not a mild-climate default.

Measured and installed by the same team

The people who measure your openings are accountable for how the finished install performs — sealing, flashing, and finish work included.

Straightforward estimates

Pricing explained in person, in the language you're most comfortable with — no pressure to decide on the spot.
